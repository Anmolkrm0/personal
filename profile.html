05 / AI product principles

AI is not the feature. The workflow is the product.

01

Start with the user's decision.

The model should serve the job the user needs to complete, not the other way around.

02

Accuracy depends on the workflow.

The right quality bar changes with the consequence of being wrong.

03

Keep humans where they matter.

Consequential conclusions need appropriate human accountability and review.

04

AI evaluation is a product responsibility.

Output quality, validation, feedback, and model performance belong in the product loop.

05

Make uncertainty understandable.

Good AI UX helps people understand what the system knows and where it may be uncertain.

06

Prompting is one layer.

Retrieval, context, workflow design, evaluation, and UX all contribute to useful AI.
